# Displays
A Display allows you to play one or multiple Scene Playlists simultaneously. To make one, click the `+` from the Scene Picker (Home) and select `Display`. This will bring you to the Display page. When you return to the Home page, you will see your new display in the list of Displays.

The Display feature is a replacement for the Grid and Scene Overlay features.
It is a composition of one or multiple Views. Each view has `x`, `y` and `z` coordinates and `width`, `height` and `opacity` properties.

### Sync/Mirror Views
You can also sync playback to another View by choosing the `Sync` option and then choosing which View to `Sync With`. Optionally you can choose to `Mirror` the View horizontally or vertically.
